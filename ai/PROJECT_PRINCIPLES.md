# Engineering Principles

---

# Purpose

These principles guide every design and implementation decision in this project.

Whenever a design decision is unclear, consult this document first.

---

## Principle 1

Design before implementation.

---

## Principle 2

Business logic must never depend on infrastructure.

---

## Principle 3

Every class should have a single responsibility.

---

## Principle 4

Every abstraction must solve a real problem.

---

## Principle 5

Prefer readability over cleverness.

---

## Principle 6

Never build for imaginary requirements.

(YAGNI)

---

## Principle 7

Keep APIs small and focused.

---

## Principle 8

Prefer simple solutions.

(KISS)

---

## Principle 9

Document important architectural decisions.

---

## Principle 10

Understand every line before writing the next.

---
## Principle 11

A package is a design decision, not just a folder.

A package should answer:

"What responsibility do these classes share?"

Not:

"Where can I put this file?"
